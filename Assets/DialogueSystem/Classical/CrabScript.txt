ASTRONAUT
Greetings! I have come to your planet from far away as an ambassador representing the planet GDM.


CRAB
Hello and welcome to Classico, ambassador! Or as we say on this planet, “hello and welcome to Classico, ambassador” [using “The Calling” font] ↓  


ASTRONAUT
Thank you! It seems a little… deserted and sad. No offence.


CRAB
You’re right. The soil is unfertile and a high number of trees have been chopped down. A lot of inhabitants have left in search of a better star system, and those that have stayed don’t go out much. 


CRAB
It’s dangerous these days…


ASTRONAUT
Oh no! What happened?


CRAB
A terrible evil has taken over this starsystem. The ruler of this planet, Bachovenpinanoff, who was once kind and just, has brought our world to the brink of death. They live in the castle behind me.


ASTRONAUT
Why not rebel against them?


CRAB
There are not many of us left, and I am but a puny servant. There is nothing I could do, except hope for better days. 


ASTRONAUT
Maybe I could help, then?


CRAB
That would be too much to ask. Seeing the resoluteness on your face. Oh, but if you insist. To get to the castle, you must obtain two keys. 


CRAB
The first one is on the road to the left and is guarded by a super vigilant android. They have but one weakness: they close their eyes to rest, but in an unpredictable pattern. 


CRAB
Get past them, and you will obtain the first key.  Come see me once you have done so. Good luck.


ASTRONAUT
Thank you. I shall try my best.


>
SECOND KEY


ASTRONAUT
I have succeeded! Here is the first key.


CRAB
Congratulations, I knew you could do it! Now, for the second key, you must pass the training I have created for you. This will give you the necessary preparation to beat Classico’s ruler. 


CRAB
It can be found at the end of the road to the right. Come back to see me once you have finished it.


>
COMPLETION


ASTRONAUT
I have completed your training. I feel ready to face Classico’s ruler.


CRAB
Perfect. Before you depart, do you have any questions for me?


ASTRONAUT
I was wondering, what happened to the ruler of Classico?


CRAB
You see, life in our star system rests on music. Once, this was a prosperous land, filled with its delicate sound. But one day, our ruler got corrupted by the Great Evil. 


CRAB
This evil has taken our music away causing us to lose our ways. The same happened to our neighbours, on Popula’. Our whole star system is dying.


ASTRONAUT
What is this Great Evil?


CRAB
Shivers I cannot say…


CRAB
If you are ready, you may enter the castle. I wish you luck, for all of our sakes.
